## 2026-11-07 — analytics: the video detail reads only the asked videos; the replay rate is estimated over 90 days (decision #144, issue #505)

On prod (pPUsRuZitL3f, about 43 days of data), the replay-sorted, searched videos table was the slowest video statement: 7.8 s, 1.2 GiB, spilling. The detail panel rebuilt the whole key map to answer one video (680 MiB). Both changes are in `VideoQueryReader` (engine-13.md §3.1, "Read cost").

- **VIDEO filter pushdown (exact).** A VIDEO `IN` term narrows every key map of the statement, the filter's and the joined one, to the ids that have a `TVideoDaily` row whose `VideoKey`, or its cut, is a filtered key. The key rule then runs over all rows of those ids, so every id's key is unchanged. There is no narrowing when a key is `UNIDENTIFIED` or the term is not `IN`. `videoFields` is narrowed the same way by the returned keys, on both sides of its INNER JOIN (the field rows and the map). The prod cost gate (cg-153806 vs cg-134336, pPUsRuZitL3f) measured the detail panel's reads R13-11d/p/q/t at about 0.7 s and 680 MiB before, about 0.04 s and 0 MiB after; the field read was narrowed after that run.
- **Sampled replay over 90 days (decision #144).** A window of more than `REPLAY_EXACT_MAX_DAYS` (90) UTC days counts the replay over `cityHash64(CidUserChecksum) % REPLAY_SAMPLE_MODULUS (8) = 0`. Unkeyed, this is a WHERE predicate. Keyed, the other users fold into one NULL user per key, so a replay-sorted table keeps every video, `total` included. A sampled row whose base is under `REPLAY_SAMPLE_FLOOR` (30) returns `replayRate: null` (`videoMeasures(c, replaySampled)`). Each window decides on its own length, a comparison window too: a CUSTOM or PREVIOUS_YEAR comparison window of a different length may take the other branch. Ranges of 90 days or less are exact, as before. Measured: the site replay at 365 days fell from 2.6–3.1 s and 1.04 GiB to 0.5 s and 332 MiB. The keyed R13-3s replay at 365 days fell from 9.8 s to 4.3 s, and from 1.25 to 1.05 GiB. The `replayRate` description and the screen document's client rule ("(est.)", "N/A, too few users") are updated.
- **No search pushdown.** A key-level search pushdown (a `vhit` window column on the key map, rows restricted before the grouping) was built and measured. It showed no gain: the gate's term `a` matches nearly every key, and R13-3s at 90 days went from 10.1 s to 11.6 s, with 55 spills instead of 31. It was dropped, and the search stays a HAVING, as before.
- **Rollback floor:** any older image (read path only, no schema).
