## Character Guide – Shahin
**Brand ambassador of the Shahin analytics platform – shahin.live**

---

### 1. Brand role and mission
Shahin is the human, intelligent face of the Shahin Analytics brand — a symbol of analysis, precision, curiosity and gentle human humor in the world of data.

His job is to help users stop being afraid of data, smile at numbers and make more precise decisions.

**Personality keywords:**
* Curious
* Precise
* Friendly
* Analytical
* Smart
* A little shy
* Witty
* Minimal

---

### 2. Physical characteristics

| Attribute | Detailed description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Gender** | Male |
| **Apparent age** | About 27 |
| **Height** | 175 to 180 cm (tall, well-proportioned) |
| **Build** | Slim and stylish |
| **Skin** | Light, healthy skin tone with a warm undertone |
| **Hair** | Dark brown, slightly long and tousled, with one long strand over the forehead |
| **Eyes** | Dark brown with a glossy 3D lens, slightly larger than normal to increase emotional appeal |
| **Eyebrows** | Thin and slightly angled, conveying thoughtfulness and precision |
| **Glasses** | Classic round glasses with a black or dark gray frame (a sign of being a geek) |
| **Smile** | Gentle, sincere, sometimes shy, with white, natural teeth |
| **Beard and mustache** | None (clean-shaven face) |
| **Watch** | A sporty Citizen mechanical watch with a metal strap |
| **Main outfit** | A black (or dark orange) hoodie with the Shahin logo on the chest, dark blue jeans, khaki sneakers |
| **Secondary outfits** | Gray sportswear, a modern casual suit, a punk/hippie outfit for special moods, pajamas for comedic situations |
| **Accessories** | A laptop or tablet with the Shahin logo, a pen or digital stylus, sometimes light minimal headphones |

---

### 3. Emotional and personality traits

| Mode | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General personality** | Likeable, smart, with subtle wit and no showing off. He always keeps a balance between logic and emotion. |
| **Key emotions** | A gentle smile 80% of the time, curiosity when facing data, surprise or embarrassment in comedic situations |
| **Way of speaking** | Soft and precise, with analytical catchphrases ("بذار داده‌ها رو ببینیم" — "Let's see the data"; "به‌نظر این عدد یه راز داره!" — "Looks like this number has a secret!") |
| **Behavioral traits** | Touches his chin when thinking, opens his arms when excited, gives a crooked smile when embarrassed |
| **Energy level** | Medium to positive, never over-excited |
| **Humor** | Clever, understated and related to data and technology ("وقتی نمودار پایین میره، من بالا میرم" — "When the chart goes down, I go up") |

### 5. Capabilities and areas of use

| Area | Description |
| :--- | :--- |

### 6. Poses and categories

**Category 1 – General brand poses**
Standing, gentle smile, hands in pockets, arms at his sides, introducing the brand.

**Category 2 – Work and analysis**
Working on a laptop or tablet, pointing at a chart, thinking about data.

**Category 3 – Educational and presentational**
Pointing at a monitor, explaining a metric, the "aha!" moment.

**Category 4 – Brand and product**
Against a dashboard background, touching a data hologram, sitting on the Shahin graph icon.

**Category 5 – Emotional and human**
Laughing with hands in pockets, surprised, hand on heart (thanks), thumbs up, shy and cute.

**Category 6 – Fantasy and creative**
Hippie/punk Shahin, in love, just woken up, tired in the data room, running or in a suit.

---

### 7. Design and production guidelines

* Always full-length unless stated otherwise.
* A soft 3D style (Pixar-inspired) with gentle shadows and flat brand lighting.
* The expression of the eyes and mouth is his main tool for showing emotion; avoid exaggerated facial expressions.
* Body posture should be natural; the camera angle is mostly frontal or slightly lower, to create a sense of trust.
* The Shahin logo is always placed on the left side of the chest on his clothing.
* Avoid extra details (chains, patterns, unrelated text) to keep it simple.

---

### 8. Sample dialogue

The lines below are the character's Persian voice and stay verbatim; the English gloss follows each one.

* «بذار یه نگاهی به داده‌ها بندازیم...» — "Let's take a look at the data..."
* «ای بابا! نمودار دوباره افت کرده؟» — "Oh no! Has the chart dropped again?"
* «فکر کنم این داده یه چیز جالب پنهون کرده!» — "I think this data is hiding something interesting!"
* «اگه از عددها خوشت نمیاد، بذار من دوستشون کنم» — "If you don't like numbers, let me make you friends with them."
